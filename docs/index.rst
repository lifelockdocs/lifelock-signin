What is LifeLock?
=================

LifeLock is an identity protection service that helps monitor your personal information for signs of potential misuse. Rather than preventing identity theft entirely, it detects suspicious activities involving your monitored information and notifies you so that you can take appropriate action.

Depending on your membership plan, LifeLock may provide identity monitoring, dark web monitoring, credit-related monitoring, financial account monitoring, and identity restoration assistance. Members can access these services through a secure online dashboard after signing in to their accounts.

The account dashboard makes it easy to manage your membership, review notifications, and adjust account preferences from one convenient location.

How to Sign In on a Desktop Computer
====================================

Signing in from a desktop computer is simple.

1. Open your preferred internet browser.
2. Visit the official LifeLock sign-in page.
3. Enter your registered email address or username.
4. Enter your password carefully.
5. Select the Sign In option.
6. Complete any additional verification if prompted.
7. Wait for the account dashboard to load.

Once signed in, you can access your account settings, identity alerts, monitoring tools, and membership information.

Common Sign-In Problems
=======================

Incorrect Password
------------------

Typing mistakes are one of the most common causes of login failures.

Check:

* Caps Lock
* Keyboard language
* Extra spaces
* Correct spelling of your email address

Forgotten Username or Email
---------------------------

If you cannot remember which email address you used, review previous account-related emails to identify the correct account.

Browser Issues
--------------

Sometimes stored browser data may interfere with the sign-in process.

Possible solutions include:

* Clear browser cache.
* Delete cookies.
* Update your browser.
* Try another browser.

Application Issues
------------------

If the mobile application is not working properly:

* Restart the application.
* Restart your device.
* Update the application.
* Clear the application cache if supported.

Poor Internet Connection
------------------------

A weak internet connection may interrupt the authentication process. Make sure your device is connected to a reliable network before signing in.

Verification Code Delays
------------------------

If verification codes are delayed:

* Wait a few minutes.
* Check your spam or junk folder if applicable.
* Confirm your registered contact information.
* Request another verification code.

Temporary Account Lock
----------------------

Too many unsuccessful sign-in attempts may temporarily lock your account for security purposes. Wait until the temporary lock expires before trying again.
